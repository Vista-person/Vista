Vision

To become a recognised leader in inclusive workplace software solutions.

Mission

To design accessible, modular software tools that assist individuals with learning difficulties in professional environments.

How Vista works

Five design choices shape every part of the product.

Adaptive by design

The interface adjusts to individual needs, such as simpler layouts, clearer reading support and fewer distractions.

Modular add-ons

Tools sit on top of the software people already use, so employers do not have to replace their systems.

Privacy first

Personal information is protected through privacy-by-design measures and data-protection rules such as GDPR where they apply.

Accessibility standards

Development follows recognised guidance such as WCAG 2.2.

Phased and partner-led

Vista plans to grow step by step with established platform partners, testing with users before wider release.

The planned path

Vista will validate each stage before moving to the next.

1. Sandbox and security review

Test integrations internally and audit privacy and security.

2. Closed developer preview

Share early builds with a small group to gather technical feedback.

3. Controlled pilot

Work with a partner organisation to check performance, compliance and real use.

Current stage: Vista is at its formation stage. The product, partnerships and plans on this page are proposals, not completed launches.
